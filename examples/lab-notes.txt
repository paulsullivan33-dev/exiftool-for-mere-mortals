The shoebox, specimen by specimen
==================================

Built by build-shoebox.sh. Seven JPEGs and
one GPX track. The pictures are minimal
synthesised JPEGs (flat colours, no real
scene); the metadata is the specimen, and
it was written by ExifTool itself in the
builder's single -overwrite_original pass
per file. Names, people, and dates are
invented; the coordinates are public
places, never anyone's home.

harbour-morning.jpg
  120x90. Canon EOS 80D. Full EXIF: taken
  2023:06:14 07:42:18 (all three EXIF dates
  agree), ISO 200, f/8, 1/250, 24 mm.
  Artist "A. Rivera"; ImageDescription
  "Fishing boats in the harbour at first
  light". No GPS. The guide's reference
  photograph: chapters 1, 2, 3, 10 read and
  write it.

birthday-cake.jpg
  96x96. iPhone 12, Software 17.3. Taken
  2024:02:09 19:05:41; ISO 1250, f/1.6.
  Artist "A. Rivera", a UserComment, and
  GPS at 47.6062, -122.3321 (the Seattle
  waterfront) with altitude 12 m. The
  privacy specimen: chapters 3, 8, and 10
  copy it before touching it, and chapter
  8 strips the copies.

clock-wrong.jpg
  100x75. Nikon D3500. All three EXIF
  dates read 2024:09:02 03:14:00; the
  story in its ImageDescription is that
  the camera clock was still on home time,
  1 hour 05 minutes behind the real
  moment. Chapter 5 shifts it to 04:19.
  Also chapter 6's collision pair (two
  copies, one date) and chapter 13's
  locked-file fixture.

trail-summit.jpg
  100x75. Sony ILCE-6400. Taken
  2024:05:18 11:23:40, ISO 100, f/9,
  1/320, 18 mm, with a description — and
  no GPS at all. Chapter 7 geotags it
  from trail.gpx; chapter 6 files it
  under by-date/2024-05.

scan-1994.jpg
  88x110. A "scan": its only metadata is
  EXIF Software "Flatbed Scan Utility
  2.1" and an XMP Description, "Scanned
  from a print, summer 1994". No EXIF
  dates of any kind. Chapter 1's
  near-empty read; chapter 5 sets its
  DateTimeOriginal outright; chapter 9's
  -if audit finds it.

parade-01.jpg
  120x84. Canon EOS 80D, taken
  2024:07:20 10:15:03, ISO 320, f/6.3,
  1/500, GPS at 47.6092, -122.3410, with
  the description both frames share. The
  donor of chapter 4.

parade-02.jpg
  104x78. The same moment after a
  diary-dropping editor: only the shared
  ImageDescription (plus the structural
  boilerplate any JPEG write leaves).
  No date, camera, exposure, or position.
  Chapter 4's patient; chapter 9's second
  dateless specimen.

trail.gpx
  Hand-written GPX 1.1, four trackpoints
  near 46.85, -121.76 (a national-park
  ridge), 2024-05-18 from 11:05:00Z to
  11:41:00Z, elevations 1320.4 to
  1530.0 m. The trail photo's 11:23:40
  falls between the second and third
  points — 47.2% of the way — so chapter
  7's interpolated position can be
  checked by hand. The builder copies
  this file into the shoebox unchanged.
